Northwind Logistics — Employee Handbook
Section 1: Working at Northwind

Northwind Logistics is a fictional freight brokerage founded in 2011 and
headquartered in Leeds. We coordinate road and rail freight for mid-sized
manufacturers across the UK and northern Europe. This handbook is sample
data used to demonstrate the RAG Generator; none of it describes a real
company.

Working hours. Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core
hours you are free to arrange your day as you see fit, provided your work
is covered and your calendar is accurate. Operations staff on the dispatch
rota work a fixed 07:00 to 15:30 shift, because carriers call early and
someone must always answer.

Remote work. Northwind is remote-first. Every role except dispatch and
warehouse liaison can be performed from anywhere within three hours of UK
time. Employees who wish to work from outside that band for more than four
consecutive weeks need written approval from their manager and from People
Operations, mainly so we can handle payroll and tax correctly.

Office space. We keep two small offices, in Leeds and Rotterdam. Desks are
not assigned; book one through the internal booking tool at least a day
ahead. Meeting rooms may be booked for up to three hours at a time. The
Leeds office is open from 08:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and is closed at
weekends unless a dispatch incident is running.

Equipment. New starters receive a laptop, a monitor, a headset and a chair
allowance of £400. Replacement laptops are issued on a three-year cycle, or
sooner if the machine fails. Damaged equipment should be reported to IT
Support the same day; we do not deduct the cost of accidental damage from
salary.
