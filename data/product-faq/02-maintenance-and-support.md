# Tidewater Sensors — Product FAQ

## Maintenance, Calibration and Support

**How often should the sensor be calibrated?**
Calibrate the pH and dissolved oxygen probes every six months under normal
conditions, or every three months in high-sediment water. Temperature and
turbidity are factory calibrated and do not need routine adjustment.

**How do I calibrate a probe?**
Rinse the probe head in deionised water, place it in the supplied buffer
solution, and start the calibration routine from the device screen in the
Tidewater app. The routine takes about four minutes per probe and must not
be interrupted. Calibration certificates are stored against the device
record automatically.

**How do I clean a fouled probe?**
Soak the probe head in the supplied cleaning solution for ten minutes, then
wipe it with the soft brush in the maintenance kit. Never use abrasive pads
or solvents; they scratch the optical window on the turbidity sensor and
readings drift permanently afterwards.

**What does a flashing red status light mean?**
A slow red flash indicates a failed reading on at least one probe, usually
fouling or an air bubble trapped against the membrane. A fast red flash
means the battery is below 5 percent. A solid red light means the firmware
has failed to boot and the unit needs to be returned.

**What is covered by the warranty?**
Tidewater sensors carry a two-year warranty covering manufacturing defects,
including water ingress through a sealed housing. The warranty does not
cover physical damage from impact, probe fouling, or batteries, which are
treated as consumables.

**How do I get support?**
Support is available by email on weekdays between 08:00 and 18:00 UTC, with
a target first response of one business day. Customers on the Assured plan
also get a 24-hour emergency line and next-business-day replacement
hardware.
